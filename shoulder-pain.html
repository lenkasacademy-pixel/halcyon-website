Conditions We Treat

Expert Care for Every Shoulder Condition

Our specialists treat a wide range of shoulder conditions using evidence-based, non-surgical methods.

❄️ Frozen Shoulder

Progressive stiffness and pain that limits all shoulder movement. Often worse at night and during routine activities.

Hydrodilatation Ultrasound-guided injection Physiotherapy
🔄 Rotator Cuff Injuries

Tears or tendinitis in the rotator cuff cause weakness and pain when lifting or rotating the arm.

PRP Therapy Guided injection Rehabilitation
💎 Calcific Tendinitis

Calcium deposits in the rotator cuff cause sudden, severe pain. Often mismanaged with just painkillers.

Barbotage Ultrasound-guided aspiration
🔴 Shoulder Bursitis & Impingement

Inflamed bursa causes pain with arm movement, especially overhead. Very common and highly treatable.

Bursa injection Ultrasound guidance
🦴 Shoulder Arthritis

Joint degeneration causing grinding, pain, and reduced range of motion. Manageable without joint replacement.

Viscosupplementation Intra-articular injection
⚡ Chronic Shoulder Pain

Persistent pain without clear diagnosis. We perform thorough evaluation to identify and treat the root cause.

Comprehensive assessment Targeted treatment

Why Ultrasound-Guided?

  • Precise needle placement into the exact problem area
  • Safer — avoids nerves and blood vessels
  • More effective than landmark-based injections
  • Real-time visualization during the procedure
  • Reduced discomfort and faster recovery
